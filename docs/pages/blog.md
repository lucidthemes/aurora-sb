## Routes

### Blog

Used to show the list of all blog posts

#### Route

`/blog`

### Category

Used to show the list of blog posts within a category

#### Route

`/blog/category/photography`

### Tag

Used to show the list of blog posts within a tag

#### Route

`/blog/tag/beach`

### Author

Used to show the list of blog posts by an author

#### Route

`/blog/author/lucid-themes`

### Search

Used to show the list of blog posts from the results of a search

#### Route

`/search?term=fashion`
